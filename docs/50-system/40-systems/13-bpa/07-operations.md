# BPA Standard Governance — Operations

The Standards Steward (PER-StandardsSteward) runs the change process, releases and
certification. Until an independent body exists, the steward role is held by a named group in
the UBOS organisation with a published conflict-of-interest policy; third-party implementers
get equal access to proposals and vectors. The certification environment is a reproducible
infrastructure definition published with each release.
